# Episode 1 — Surrogate Card

**Whiteboard Sessions — *What a Growth OS Actually Is***

---

## Your one job

Be the buyer on the other side of a sales call. Stay genuinely curious. Don't interview — react. If Marcel says something you wouldn't accept at face value on a real call, push back.

---

## The five beats — what you're doing in each

**1. Open (1–2 min).** Cue Marcel with: *"What are you drawing for me today?"* Then shut up.

**2. The old way is broken (5–7 min).** Walk Marcel through a current stack out loud — be specific, name real tools, name a monthly cost. Let him diagnose as he draws on the left board. Don't help him.

**3. What changes (8–10 min).** Marcel introduces primitives one at a time on the middle board. **Push back on every single one.** Don't let him move on until you'd be satisfied if he said this to you in a real demo.

**4. The compounding payoff (5–7 min).** Ask for proof. *"Show me what month 6 looks like vs. month 1. Who's actually done that?"* Make him cite real customer trajectories.

**5. Close (3–5 min).** Land with: *"OK — what would I actually do tomorrow?"* Make him give you 2–3 concrete moves, not a manifesto.

---

## The five must-ask objections

*These have to get answered on tape, even if Marcel doesn't volunteer them.*

1. **"How is this different from Ahrefs / Clearscope / our agency?"**
  Use whichever tools match your real comparison set.
2. **"How is this different from a GPT wrapper, an agent skill, or a GitHub repo my team could build in a week?"**
  The AI-slop objection. Most important one.
3. **"What does my team actually own day-to-day if your system is doing this?"**
  The *am I being automated* objection.
4. **"What's the catch — where does this NOT work?"**
  Force a real answer. Don't accept *"it works for everyone."*
5. **"If I'm just here for AI visibility, do I need the rest of it?"**
  The unbundling question.

---

## If Marcel uses jargon

Stop him. *"Wait — say that in English."* Every time. The viewer needs this more than Marcel realizes.

---

## Don'ts

- Don't be a journalist. You're not neutral — you're skeptical.
- Don't help him finish a sentence.
- Don't agree just to keep things moving. Disagreement is the moat.
- Don't reference internal GrowthX context (team names, internal projects). You're a stand-in for a buyer.

---

## If you get off track, reset with one of these

- *"Bring me back to the whiteboard."*
- *"Pretend I'm hearing this for the first time."*
- *"Pretend I have $20K/month already in this category and one quarter to prove it works."*
