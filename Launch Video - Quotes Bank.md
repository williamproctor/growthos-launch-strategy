*Why it's #3: "Unfair advantage that every marketer wishes they had" is the closing-frame sound bite. Best candidate to end the video on, or end Beat 3 before Beat 4 takes over.*

### 4. Why closed-loop requires all the signals
**[00:20:44 → 00:21:23]**

*Why it's #4: This is the visual / B-roll script — every signal he names = an icon on screen. Long for a 90s cut; perfect for the homepage product page or whiteboard session.*

### 7. The learning loop, in plain English

*Why it's #7: The clearest plain-English explanation of how the closed loop *learns*. Critical for the "what makes this different" beat.*

## Beat 4 — The new stakes (AEO / AI visibility)

*Why it's #2: "How do I play inception with these agents?" is the sound bite of the video. Memorable, founder-voiced, instantly visual.*

### 3. "Synthesizing the entire web" + the new game's rules
